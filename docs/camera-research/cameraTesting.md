# Camera Testing

## NOTES:
- Camera used was not a traditional webcam...
	- Mine stopped working and the next best thing I had that wasn't my built in webcam was an older Canon EOS Rebel XS that I needed to download an external virtual webcam application for. This, in turn, could have impacted this experiment slightly.
	- The FPS may not be as good as it is with the DSLR camera. When testing with my webcam and my external webcam (before it broke on me), I was getting an average FPS of 28-30.

## Five Minute Experiment Results
- Taking a picture of the timer and the doctor view, there is a 0.68ms difference. This high latency could be the result of the external webcam application I had to use with my camera.
- The minimum camera delay was 3.05ms 
- The maximum camera delay was 66.37ms
- The average camera delay was 21.62ms
- There were 15370 frames captured, and over five minutes (+2min 50s for set-up/take-down time), the average frame rate was 32.70fps
- The average jitter was 10.11ms for capture jitter and 12.05ms for recieving jitter.
